How I measure

Every morning, a collector gathers the project-management assignments published in France on freelance platforms, keeping only freelance listings. The displayed rate is the median of announced rates — not an average, which a single extreme figure would distort. Listings without a rate do not count in the median, but do count in the volumes. The measurement is redone at every publication: this page is never a memory.
