Freelance project-management day rate in Paris and Île-de-France

Across the project-management assignment ads collected in Paris and Île-de-France, the median day rate stands at 600 € per day. That figure rests on 754 ads publishing a rate, out of 1,596 ads collected in this area.

What the gap with the national market means

This area pays 50 € more per day than the national median (550 €). Over a year of 200 billed days, that gap is 10,000 €.

The middle half of priced ads in this area falls between 520 € and 654 €. Below the first quartile a rate invites questions; above the third it needs a scope to justify it.

02

Remote work in this area

Of the 878 ads in this area that state their arrangement, 26 are fully remote, i.e. 3 %. The rest split between hybrid and on-site.

05

How I measure

Every morning, a collector gathers the project-management assignments published in France on freelance platforms, keeping only freelance listings. The displayed rate is the median of announced rates — not an average, which a single extreme figure would distort. Listings without a rate do not count in the median, but do count in the volumes. The measurement is redone at every publication: this page is never a memory.
