When to call me

Your programme is slipping mid-course

The go-live date is approaching and the plan no longer holds. I come in mid-flight: I rebuild a single authoritative plan, restore a steering committee that actually decides, and either hold the date or call the new one early enough to be credible.

Your portfolio is unreadable

Nobody can say what is running, what is late, or what it will cost. I qualify, prioritise and get decisions made — then industrialise the governance so it holds without me.

Your IT budget does not hold

A budget built under pressure, CAPEX and OPEX mixed, a roadmap nobody can verify. I frame it from scratch if needed, install a single arbitration grid, and ring-fence what has to land.

What I do

I take on projects that need someone steering, not someone observing.

  • Project and programme direction — framing, milestones, steering committees, arbitration. Governance that holds when the schedule moves.
  • PMO and portfolio governance — forums, indicators, executive reporting. Making what is running legible.
  • Project finance — budget, CAPEX/OPEX, management control. The cost trajectory as much as the schedule.
  • Transformation and change management — ERP, HR information systems, data migration, target organisation.

The market record

The record is my own market watch, made public. Every day I collect the published project-management assignments, follow each one until it disappears, and publish the figures — day rates, time to disappear, remote work, regions.

Why publish it: because nobody else does. A client hiring a PMO has no idea what the market costs or how fast it moves. Neither does a freelancer. Every figure is published with its limits — that is what makes it defensible.

Working together

Available for assignments in the Paris region, in northern France, or fully remote. Describe the context in three lines and I will tell you plainly whether I am the right person.
