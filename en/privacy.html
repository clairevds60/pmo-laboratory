Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

In short: this site uses no cookies, no trackers and no analytics, and contains no forms. You can read it without leaving any data.

What this site collects

No personal data is collected by the site: no account, no form, no analytics cookie. The host keeps technical logs (IP address, timestamp) for security and availability, according to its own retention periods.

If you book a meeting

The “Book 30 minutes” button is an outbound link to Calendly, a third-party service. No Calendly script is loaded on this site: until you click, no data leaves. If you do book, the information you enter is processed by Calendly under its own privacy policy and passed to me so the meeting can take place.

If you email me

Your address and the content of your message are kept in my professional mailbox for the duration of our exchange and the commercial follow-up arising from it. They are never sold or passed to third parties.

Social media publishing application

The application used to publish to LinkedIn and TikTok is a personal tool, not open to the public:

  • It publishes only to Claire Van De Sype’s accounts, after her explicit authorisation.
  • It reads, collects and stores no data belonging to other users of those platforms.
  • The access token is kept locally, on her own machine, and is transmitted to no third party.
  • Authorisation can be revoked at any time from the settings of the account concerned.

Measurement data

The record is built from public job and assignment listings. The published figures are aggregated: they contain no personal names and no contact details. No listing is reproduced on this site.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of data concerning you by writing to claire.vandesype@pmo-laboratory.com. You will receive a reply within one month. You may also refer the matter to the CNIL, the French data protection authority.
